Boss
& Boss

You own a 12-person company in Shah Alam. You also are the HR department. Every week, someone walks in with a problem. Swipe left or right to decide.

💰 CashRun out and the bank takes the keys. Too much and you get bought out.
🙂 MoraleToo low: everyone quits. Too high: they run the place without you.
⚖️ ComplianceToo low: the Labour Department shuts you down. Too high: buried in paperwork.
📣 ReputationToo low: nobody applies. Too high: you become a LinkedIn guru and neglect the business.

Goal: keep the company alive for 3 years. Keep all four in balance. A dot above a meter shows it will change. Bigger dot, bigger change. It doesn't say which way.

About & disclaimer

Not legal advice. Boss & Boss is a game for learning and discussion. Its employment rules are simplified, based on Malaysian law as of 2025, and may be out of date or incomplete. For a real workplace issue, check the current law or ask the Labour Department (JTK) or a qualified lawyer.

Fiction. All characters, companies and situations are made up. Any resemblance to real people or companies is coincidence. Laws, agencies and schemes named in the game (such as JTK, CIDB, Bank Negara, MSPO) are real, but none of them made, checked or endorse this game.

Privacy. The game collects nothing and sends nothing. Your progress stays on this device. A result code holds only your scores, your choices and any name you typed, and goes only where you send it.
