Boss & Boss
Game-based HR & compliance session for Malaysian workplaces

The problem

Employment-law and people-management training is important, but slide decks are forgotten by lunch. Managers rarely practise the actual decisions.

The session

Participants run a company in a swipe game. Every week, a staff member walks in with a real Malaysian workplace situation. Every choice moves cash, morale, compliance and reputation.

How it runs (45 minutes)

5 min
Intro: you're the boss and the HR
15 min
Play on own phone, same situations for all
3 min
Send results to trainer by WhatsApp
22 min
Live debrief on the room's choices

What the debrief shows

  • Which legal shortcuts people took, and what the law says
  • Decisions where the room split, for discussion
  • Hidden hiring-bias patterns (age, gender, origin…)
  • Each person's HR style: people-first vs profit-first
  • CSV summary for your training records

Topics covered

  • Working hours & overtime, rest days
  • EPF, minimum wage, wage payment
  • Maternity, paternity, flexible work
  • Harassment complaints, misconduct, inquiries
  • Safety (OSHA), data (PDPA), fair retrenchment

Type

Bricolage Grotesque: headlines (ExtraBold) and text.
Kalam: speech bubbles and comic captions only.

Characters

Hand-drawn ink and watercolour, newspaper-cartoon style. Characters always face the reader and smile or react; never mock a group.

Voice

  • Warm, funny, observational about office life
  • Light Manglish: “can or not”, “lah”, “boss”
  • Honest: “simplified, not legal advice”
  • Never jokes about race, religion, gender or disability
  • Never claims HRD Corp claimability until confirmed
