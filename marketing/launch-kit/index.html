1. Strategy in one minute

🎯 GoalPlayers spread the game for free. The real win is HR and L&D managers booking workshops.
👥 PlayersMalaysian working adults, 22–45. They share funny, relatable things on WhatsApp and TikTok.
💼 BuyersHR managers, L&D, SME owners. Reach them personally on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email.
📣 Where1. WhatsApp (status, groups, one-to-one) · 2. LinkedIn (buyers) · 3. TikTok / Reels (reach) · 4. Facebook (SME owners 35+)
🪝 The hook“Which ending did you get?” Invite people to compare endings and tag “the Kak Ros / Uncle Lim in your office”.
⏰ WhenPost 12–1pm or 8–10pm Malaysia time. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

Golden rules

  • Send buyer messages one by one, with their name. No bulk blasts.
  • Offer 2–3 free pilot sessions first, then use their feedback as testimonials (with permission).
  • Say “simplified, not legal advice”. Don't claim HRD Corp claimability until you've confirmed it.
  • Use a link you can count clicks on (e.g. a short link), because the game itself tracks nothing.
  • Track: link clicks, comments, DMs, and the number of workshop enquiries. That last one is what matters.
